01 A better way to build culture

Culture begins in small moments

Culture grows from the moments people choose to notice.

Every team has meaningful contributions that deserve to be seen—a colleague stepping in, an idea moving the company forward, or someone quietly making work better for everyone.

When those moments are recognized, people feel connected to something larger. Appreciation becomes more than praise. It becomes evidence of what the company values.

What a company celebrates today becomes the culture it carries forward tomorrow.
02 The culture gap

Important work is becoming invisible

Great work happens every day. Too much of it disappears unnoticed.

Appreciation is often trapped in private messages, remembered too late, or reduced to a generic “good job.” Over time, meaningful contributions become invisible—and people begin to wonder whether their effort matters.

01

Contributions disappear

Important work is completed, but the people behind it are not always recognized.

02

Appreciation becomes inconsistent

Recognition depends on individual managers instead of becoming a shared company habit.

03

Rewards feel disconnected

Rewards are given occasionally, without a clear connection to the contribution that earned them.

04

Culture becomes difficult to see

Leaders want to build belonging, but lack a clear picture of how appreciation is experienced.

Culture cannot be strengthened by values written on a wall. It grows through the behaviors people experience repeatedly.
