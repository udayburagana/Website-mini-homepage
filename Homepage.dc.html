01 The Problem

Important work is becoming invisible

The work that moves companies forward rarely fits inside a performance review.

It happens when someone stays back to help a teammate.
When an employee solves a customer problem before it escalates.
When a new hire asks the question everyone else was avoiding.

When a manager quietly keeps the team moving through a difficult week.
These contributions shape culture—but most disappear inside meetings, messages and memory.

Meaningful contributions are remembered during annual reviews, if they are remembered at all.

⌗

⌗

Rewards feel disconnected

Gift cards and incentives are distributed, but the contribution behind them is often forgotten.

⌗

Culture remains difficult to see

Leaders know culture matters, but have little visibility into how appreciation actually moves through the company.

09 Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining early access.

What is EzRewards?

EzRewards is an employee recognition and rewards platform that helps companies make everyday contributions visible, celebrate meaningful work and manage rewards through one connected experience.

Is EzRewards only a gift-card platform?

No. Rewards are one part of the experience. EzRewards connects recognition, public appreciation, rewards and culture reporting so companies can build a continuous appreciation system—not simply distribute vouchers.

Who is EzRewards designed for?

EzRewards is designed primarily for growing companies with 30 or more employees that want to build a more visible and participative workplace culture.

Can any employee recognize another employee?

Yes. EzRewards is built around company-wide, peer-to-peer recognition. Employees can appreciate colleagues across teams and departments.

Does every recognition include a monetary reward?

No. Recognition can be completely non-monetary. Employees can appreciate colleagues through meaningful messages and recognition badges. Rewards can be assigned separately when appropriate.

How does pricing work?

EzRewards costs $1 per active employee each month. Pending invitations do not consume seats. When an additional employee activates their account, the subscription quantity increases automatically and the additional seat is billed proportionately for the remaining billing period.

Is the reward budget included in the subscription?

No. Platform subscription fees and reward funding are separate. Your company decides how much to add to its reward wallet and how those funds are distributed.

How do employees join?

Companies can onboard employees using Microsoft SSO or structured CSV upload. Invited employees can then activate their accounts and complete their profiles.

Is EzRewards available now?

EzRewards is currently accepting applications for early access. Join the waitlist and we will contact you as onboarding slots become available.

Can any employee recognize another employee?

Yes. Employees recognize colleagues across teams and departments without administrator approval.

Does every recognition include a monetary reward?

No. Recognition can remain completely non-monetary. Administrators assign rewards separately when needed.

How does pricing work?

EzRewards costs $1 per active employee each month. Pending invitations do not consume seats.

What happens if we invite more employees than our current seat count?

Only active users count. The subscription quantity increases automatically as more employees activate accounts.

Is the reward budget included in the subscription?

No. Platform fees and reward funding are separate. Your company manages the wallet independently.

How do employees join?

Administrators onboard employees through Microsoft SSO or structured CSV upload.

What can administrators manage?

Administrators can manage employee access, roles, active seats, the reward catalogue, reward assignments, wallet visibility and structured reports from EzRewards.

Is EzRewards available now?

EzRewards is accepting early-access applications. We contact applicants as onboarding slots become available.
