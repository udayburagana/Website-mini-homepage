01 A better way to build culture

Culture begins in small moments

Culture grows from the moments people choose to notice.

Every team has meaningful contributions that deserve to be seen—a colleague stepping in, an idea moving the company forward, or someone quietly making work better for everyone.

When those moments are recognized, people feel connected to something larger. Appreciation becomes more than praise. It becomes evidence of what the company values.

What a company celebrates today becomes the culture it carries forward tomorrow.
02 The culture gap

Important work is becoming invisible

Great work happens every day. Too much of it disappears unnoticed.

Appreciation is often trapped in private messages, remembered too late, or reduced to a generic “good job.” Over time, meaningful contributions become invisible—and people begin to wonder whether their effort matters.

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers for growing teams.

Everything you need to understand the platform, participation model, rewards and early access.

What is EzRewards?

EzRewards is an employee recognition and rewards platform that connects peer recognition, company-wide appreciation, reward management and structured reporting through one system.

Is EzRewards only a gift-card platform?

No. Rewards are one component. The platform connects the contribution, company-wide appreciation, any reward that follows and the reporting administrators need.

Who is EzRewards designed for?

Growing companies with 30 or more employees that want a more structured, visible approach to recognition and rewards.

Can any employee recognize another employee?

Yes. EzRewards supports company-wide peer-to-peer recognition across teams and departments without an administrative approval step.

Does every recognition include a monetary reward?

No. Recognition can be completely non-monetary. Administrators can assign rewards separately when appropriate.

How does pricing work?

EzRewards costs $1 per active employee each month. Pending invitations do not consume seats, and newly activated seats are billed proportionately.

Is the reward budget included in the subscription?

No. Platform subscription and reward funding are separate. Your company controls its reward wallet and distribution.

What reporting is available?

Structured recognition and reward reports are available to administrators. The AI Report Assistant answers questions using the data within those reports.

How do employees join?

Companies onboard employees using Microsoft SSO or structured CSV upload. Invited employees activate their accounts and complete their profiles.

Is EzRewards available now?

EzRewards is accepting applications for early access. Join the waitlist and we will contact you as onboarding slots become available.
