The problem

Appreciation is happening. But most companies cannot see it.

A teammate goes beyond their role. A manager solves a crisis. A team ships something difficult. Then the moment disappears into chats, calls, emails, and memory — while HR manages appreciation through spreadsheets and follow-ups.

01

Recognition is scattered

Great work gets acknowledged in chats, calls, and meetings — but rarely becomes part of company culture.

02

Rewards are manual

Vouchers, budgets, approvals, and redemptions depend on spreadsheets and back-and-forth coordination.

03

Visibility is limited

Leaders know rewards are being distributed, but cannot easily see participation, trends, or impact.

EzRewards brings it all together.
