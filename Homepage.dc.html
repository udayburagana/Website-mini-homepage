01 The Problem

Important work is becoming invisible

The work that moves companies forward rarely fits inside a performance review.

It happens when someone stays back to help a teammate. When an employee solves a customer problem before it escalates. When a new hire asks the question everyone else was avoiding. When a manager quietly keeps the team moving through a difficult week. These contributions shape culture—but most disappear inside meetings, messages and memory.

Recognition arrives too late

Meaningful contributions are remembered during annual reviews, if they are remembered at all.

Signal lost
Appreciation stays private

A “thank you” inside a chat feels good for a moment, but it does not become part of the company’s shared culture.

Momentum stalled
Rewards feel disconnected

Gift cards and incentives are distributed, but the contribution behind them is often forgotten.

Future unclear
